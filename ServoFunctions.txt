Right Arm lift servo

Servo 0 = Forward Backward
Servo 1 = Left Right
Servo 2 = Body turn left right
Servo 3 = head up down
Servo 4 = head left right
Servo 5 = Right arm lift (Broken)
Servo 6 = Right arm in and out
Servo 7 = Right elbow up down
Servo 8 = Right Wrist up down
Servo 9 = Right Wrist rotate
Servo 10 = Right hand close and open
Servo 11 = Left arm lift
Servo 12 = Left arm in and out
Servo 13 = Left elbow up down
Servo 14 = Left wrist up down
Servo 15 = Left Wrist rotate
Servo 16 = Left hand close and open
